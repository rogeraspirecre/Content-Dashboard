## The answers
- High salary with little training, or low salary with strong mentorship? **Low salary, strong mentorship.** Early in your career it's not about the money, it's about the skills and experience you'll use later.
- Big name firm with a narrow role, or small firm with large responsibility? **Small firm, large responsibility.** "This one's easy." You learn more and you're more well-rounded. Big companies are great for some people, but if you have entrepreneurial drive you probably won't like it.
- Straight commission or salary? **Straight commission.** You're forced to learn quickly, you're motivated, and you control your own destiny. You don't wait for your boss to tell you if you got a 2% or a 4% raise.
- Stay in your hometown or move to a big city? **Big city.** You can always come back later. When you're young and building a career, a big city is where you need to be.
