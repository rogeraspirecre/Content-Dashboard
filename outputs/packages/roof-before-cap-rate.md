## YouTube description
When I underwrite a small industrial building, the cap rate isn't the first number I look at. The roof is. A new roof on a 30,000 square foot warehouse can run around $300,000, and I've seen buyers skip a $2,000 roof inspection and then eat that whole bill in year one.

So before I argue about price, I get the roof age in writing, I get the warranty transferred, and I price the remaining life of the roof into my offer. And if a seller won't document the roof, that tells you what you need to know.

The second thing I check is the electrical. Small manufacturers need three phase power, and if the building doesn't have it, getting the utility to bring it in can take a year. You're paying for what the building can actually do, so check that before you fall in love with the square footage.
