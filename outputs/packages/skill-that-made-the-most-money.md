## YouTube description
What's the skill that's made me the most money? Refusing to give up.

The only certainty in business is that you're going to face challenges. You're going to lose, and you're going to have massive disappointments. You have to get over it and move on. Take a couple of days, be upset, do whatever you have to do.

What stops a lot of people from getting where they want to be is what happens after the loss. When you're feeling that pain, your natural instinct is to pull back, get less aggressive, and hesitate. And when you hesitate, you're more likely to keep having bad things happen. You have to do the opposite of your instincts. Be more aggressive, be more confident, work harder, stay focused, and don't let the unfortunate things that happen to you keep you down.
