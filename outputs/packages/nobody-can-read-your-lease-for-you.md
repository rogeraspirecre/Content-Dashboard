## YouTube description
What's the most expensive mistake a business owner can make when signing a lease? Not understanding the lease themselves, and not understanding how that lease is going to affect their business.

It doesn't matter how good your broker is, or your attorney, or your architect or designer. Only a business owner understands the business at the level of the business owner. There are things you're going to catch in a lease, in the space plan, in the layout, or in the legal provisions that nobody else would. What happens if you sell your business, for example. There are a million things like that.

The truth is the risks in a lease are endless, and the risks that really matter to one business may be completely different from the ones that matter to another. Only the business owner is going to know which is which. So the business owner needs to be engaged and understand how every aspect of this complicated, long term transaction affects their business.
