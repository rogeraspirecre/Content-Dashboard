## YouTube description
The hardest part with real estate agents is getting them to spend the time to put into the CRM what's actually needed.

It starts with training, showing them what to do and why. If you do this one thing now, then when you send a client a report or someone wants an update, it's so much easier on you. Yes, you have to do these things, but it saves you a lot of time in the long run.

A lot of the automations, they don't even know are running. They do their part, and after that the automations fire and the work gets done. It's a lot better now than it was six months ago. What it still really struggles with is understanding your business, understanding how the system is set up, and being able to make judgment calls. It's not very good at that yet, but I think we're getting there.
