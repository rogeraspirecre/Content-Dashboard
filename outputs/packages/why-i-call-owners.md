## X posts
1. A property just sold and it's fully occupied. Nothing to lease, nothing to sell. That's exactly who I'm calling.
2. Ask a new owner why they bought it and what they plan to do with it. You'll learn more in five minutes than in a year of watching listings.
3. The owner with no vacancy today has vacancy in two years. Be the person they already know.
4. Trigger events beat lists. Somebody buying a building is a reason to call that isn't about you.
5. "I saw you recently bought" is a better opener than anything you'll find in a script.
6. Most brokers call about the space that's available. The business is in the space that isn't yet.
7. You're not calling to pitch. You're calling to find out what they're trying to do.
8. Every deal you close in three years starts with a call you had no reason to make today.
