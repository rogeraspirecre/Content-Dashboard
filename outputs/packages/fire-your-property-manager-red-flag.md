## YouTube description
The one red flag that means you should fire your property manager immediately is if you tell them to use new vendors and they refuse.

I wouldn't say it's common, but it happens more than you think: property managers getting kickbacks from vendors. It usually starts with the manager using the same vendor over and over and developing a close relationship with them. Then one day the vendor says, why don't I charge a little extra on this next job and give you half of it? And over time it gets worse.

You want to make sure your property manager uses a diversity of vendors. Using different vendors keeps your vendors honest and makes sure you're getting the best pricing, and not using the same one for everything reduces the chance that there's a kickback scheme going on in the background.
