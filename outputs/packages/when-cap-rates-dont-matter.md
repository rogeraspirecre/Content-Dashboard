## YouTube description
Cap rates are a way to quickly filter through deals - and knowing when they don't matter is half the skill. If you're looking at vacant buildings, the cap rate is irrelevant.

On occupied deals, as you get to know a market really well, you develop a feel for which types of buildings sell at which cap rates. So when a listing comes across your desk, you can see the cap rate on the asking price, you know what you'd do with the property if you owned it, and you can immediately gauge whether the seller is realistic.

Time is your most valuable resource. When you can spot an unrealistic seller right away, you move on and spend your time underwriting deals with sellers who are realistic - instead of wasting weeks on a building the seller wants double what it's worth.
