## YouTube description
What makes a commercial property a lot harder to sell than the owner expects is that the owner waits until there's a problem to sell it.

When everything is good, when the tenants are paying rent, when there's a lot of term left on the leases and the landlord is just cashing a check every month, they're happy. They don't want to sell. But then some tenants leave, or a tenant stops paying on time, or there are unexpected maintenance costs and things start breaking that have to be replaced. All of a sudden the owner doesn't like owning that property as much, and now they want to sell.

In reality, the vast majority of commercial real estate buyers are buying income. They're willing to pay more for that income when everything is stable, when there's a lot of term left on the leases, and when the property doesn't have major issues. So when you wait for the problems to arrive and then decide to sell, the price you get goes way down.
