## YouTube description
In this video, I answer what would make me walk away from a listing. There are two main reasons.

The first is a client who isn't willing to transact at market terms. If you set expectations for what the market lease rate or sale price is, you bring them opportunities inside those parameters, and then suddenly they don't want to do the deal, you're wasting your time. There are plenty of people out there who are happy to have you list their property and have no intention of actually selling it.

The second is a client who doesn't perform on what they say. They tell you they'll do the deal at certain terms, you go get it done at those terms, and then when it's time to sign they change their mind and don't follow through. That puts your own reputation at risk, and you can never let that happen.
