## YouTube description
As a broker, you've got to be able to advise your clients through all the noise. Your experience, the deals you've done, the things you've seen, the market knowledge that can't be extracted from ChatGPT.

So I think this is a good thing. A lot of the information gathering, the repetitive work, the minutia, will go away over time. The question is how we put ourselves in a position to win when it does.

As brokers, we should see ourselves as being compensated to use our expertise to get an outcome. We don't get paid to do busy work. So the more of that disappears and the more we can focus on getting our clients the outcome they want, the better.
