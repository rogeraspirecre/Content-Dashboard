## YouTube description
In this video I rate retirement strategies out of ten.

Trusting your kids to retire you: I won't even rate it. Terrible idea. Your kids are going to make it harder for you to retire, not easier. Maxing out your 401(k): a four. Do it consistently on a good income and it helps you retire earlier, but you should be more focused on increasing your income. A Roth IRA: a two, same idea as the 401(k) but you can't contribute as much. The advantage is you can start before you have a job that offers a 401(k).

Real estate investments: over the past 20 years this was a ten. Over the next 20 years it's a three. Real estate worked because we had decades of falling interest rates, and when rates go down, prices go up. That isn't going to happen moving forward, so buying real estate and holding it is no longer how people retire early. You have to add value and know what you're doing.

Moving countries: zero. And the FIRE movement, financial independence retire early: a one. I can't stand it. They sell a lot of fantasy, telling people to buy some real estate and do this and that and retire at 40. It isn't realistic, and unless you get very lucky you're going to be very poor retiring at 40. Building a business or a successful career takes longer than that.
