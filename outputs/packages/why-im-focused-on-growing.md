- **This is the answer to the other clip.** "The Day You Start a Company, You Stop Doing Your Job" is the problem: you end up doing things you're bad at. This is the resolution: you grow so you can hand them back. Post them as a two-parter, problem then answer.
- **Also finishes the delegation clip.** That one says commit to no longer doing it. This one says why it matters. Three clips, one coherent argument about how an owner's job changes.
- **Broad lane.** No real estate needed. Any owner with a business bigger than themselves recognizes this.
