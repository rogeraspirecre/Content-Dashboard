## YouTube description
Have you ever wondered why so many people jump into real estate, buy their first property, tell everyone about the incredible deal they found - and then realize months later that the deal they thought was amazing was never actually a good deal at all?

In most cases, it's not because they were lazy. It's not because they didn't put in enough effort. The problem is they didn't analyze the deal properly. They made guesses and assumptions without testing and challenging those assumptions enough.

The result is underwriting that's more optimistic than reality. And when reality shows up, the deal they thought was great turns out much worse than they hoped and planned.
