## YouTube description
We were very heavy on the listing side. Landlord rep, multi-tenant buildings. That means lots of inbound leads all the time, and you have to send people information quickly, you have to know who to follow up with, and you have to report to your clients.

It used to be that you'd load all the leads into Excel, and then you'd have leasing reports in Excel for each listing. So every time you had a call with a client, you'd go to the activity log in Excel and copy the leads over into those leasing reports. It was incredibly inefficient.

Then we started using a tool designed for landlord rep listings. But there was no customer relationship management aspect to it. The leads go in there, you can create a report, and you can do nothing else with it.
