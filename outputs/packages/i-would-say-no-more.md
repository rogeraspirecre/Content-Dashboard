## YouTube description
In this video, I answer what I'd do differently if I were starting Aspire today. I'd say no more.

When you're first getting started it's easy to say yes to everything, because you think you have to take every client you can get. You're new, you're just starting out, you need the business.

But when you take clients who aren't a fit, it costs you an enormous amount of wasted time and resources. Saying no is often the path to growth. Saying yes to everything just holds you back.
