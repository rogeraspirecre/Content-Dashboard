## YouTube description
What new commercial real estate investors need to understand before they buy their first property is that things are not going to go exactly as you plan. It's guaranteed. No investment goes exactly the way you laid it out in your pro forma.

So you need to have reserves, and you need to be ready to make decisions and act quickly. A lot of times problems become much bigger when you don't act quickly, and when you hope they'll go away, they get much worse.

Don't go in being naive, thinking you're going to collect money in your mailbox every month. Get ready to operate. Get ready to get dirty. Get ready to solve problems, or you may find yourself in trouble.
