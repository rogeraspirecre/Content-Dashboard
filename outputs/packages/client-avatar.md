## YouTube description
Does everybody know what a client avatar is? A client avatar is a very specific, targeted description of who your target customer is.

For Aspire, our avatar on the landlord side is business owners who own real estate. Usually entrepreneurial groups, often based in Houston or Texas, who own buildings and need help leasing them, managing them, selling them, or finding more. That doesn't mean we don't work with people out of state, and it doesn't mean we don't work with people outside Houston. But our main target is locally based entrepreneurial owners of real estate.

On the tenant side, it's locally owned businesses that lease space.
