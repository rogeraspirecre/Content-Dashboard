## YouTube description
When you own a property and have a property manager, you shouldn't feel like you're managing that person to manage the property. But there's a real difference between property management and asset management, and sometimes frustration with a property manager comes from not understanding what asset management is.

That usually comes from people who bought a property thinking it would be a passive investment that doesn't need their involvement. A property manager should be executing your vision for the property: getting things done, collecting rent, managing vendors, and making recommendations. But the property manager isn't the owner. As the owner, you still have to make decisions, especially the ones that require investing money or are more complicated.

For example, if a tenant is unhappy and there's a good reason to do something outside of what the lease provides to keep them, the property manager can't make that call for you. Property management is executing the vision for the property. Asset management is setting the vision and direction that shapes its future. They're two very different things.
