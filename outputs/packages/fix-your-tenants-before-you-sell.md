# Fix Your Tenants Before You Sell
Format: single-point clip (~45s) | Lane: owner education, seller side | Status: filmed

## Best picks
- **Title:** The Buyer Is Going to Talk to Your Tenants
- **IG caption:** Before a buyer closes on your building, they're going to call your tenants. Whatever your tenants say is what your building is worth.
- **X post:** Every buyer talks to your tenants before closing. If your tenants have complaints, you're getting a retrade or a dead deal. Fix that first.
- **Cold open:** "A buyer is going to talk to the tenants before they close on the property." Most owners don't know this happens, and it reframes the whole clip.

## The argument
- Fix unhappy tenants before you try to sell, because the buyer will talk to them before closing.
- If tenants aren't happy and have complaints, the buyer finds out. Then they back out, or they come at the last minute and ask for a huge price reduction.
- You want tenants happy, with no complaints, and you want to do everything within reason to make them speak highly of the property.
- Not just because you want to sell. That's how you add value in the first place.
- Long term tenants drive the value of the property. Happy tenants, value probably going up. Unhappy tenants, value probably going down.
- Doing right by your tenants is automatically doing the thing that maximizes your sale price whenever you decide to sell.

## YouTube description
Sellers should fix unhappy tenants before they try to sell the property, because a buyer is going to talk to the tenants before they close.

If your tenants aren't happy, if they have a bunch of complaints, the buyer is going to find out. They're either going to back out of the deal, or they're going to come to you at the last minute and ask for a huge price reduction.

You want your tenants happy. You want them to have no complaints, and you want to do everything in your power, within reason, to make them speak highly of the property. And not just because you want to sell. That's how you add value in the first place. Long term tenants drive the value of a property. If your tenants are happy, your value is probably going up. If your tenants aren't happy, your value is probably going down. By doing the right thing by your tenants, you're automatically doing the right thing for the sales price of your property whenever you decide to sell.

## EDIT NOTES
- **Lead with the buyer calling the tenants.** He opens with the advice and gives the reason second. Flip it, because the reason is the part nobody knows.
- **"Back out or ask for a huge price reduction" is the stakes line.** Put it on screen. Retrade risk is what makes a seller act.
- **Different from the retention lane.** The channel has seven or more clips about keeping tenants happy. This one is about what unhappy tenants do to your sale price, so keep the framing on valuation and due diligence or it blends into the pile.
- **One receipt would make it.** A deal where the buyer talked to tenants and the price moved. Even anonymized, a number turns this from good advice into proof. Add it to the growing list of receipts to collect on the next shoot.
- **Strong lead-gen fit.** This targets an owner thinking about selling, which is the highest value viewer on the channel. Worth testing as an ad to owners, and worth a place in the seller pitch deck.
