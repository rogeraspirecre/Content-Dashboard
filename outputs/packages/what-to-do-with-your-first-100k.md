## YouTube description
In this video, I rank the most common things people do with their first $100,000, including buying an investment property, starting a business, paying off student loans, paying off your mortgage early, buying your dream car, and getting an MBA.

My take is going to surprise people coming from a real estate guy. Real estate is a great way to grow wealth, but it's not a great way to create it. If you're starting with your first $100K, the goal is to increase your income, not to tie up your money in something that pays you a little bit over a very long time.

I also get into why paying off a low interest mortgage early is playing defense, and why working at a fast growing company beats paying for an MBA.
