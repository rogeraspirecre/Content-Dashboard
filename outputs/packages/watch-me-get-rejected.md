Live audio of a real prospect who did not consent. He's named on the call and describes a project he's building. Get his permission, or bleep the name and cut the identifying details. He's a prospect worth keeping, so the call to ask is cheaper than the alternative.

- The owner says no. He's good, he owns a couple more, he's building one right now and it's eating his lunch.
- Instead of closing out, Brandon keeps going: where are you building? What's been the challenge up there? Do you handle leasing and management in house?
- The owner opens up about being busy: "my days are going a hundred miles an hour, I don't know what's coming at me half the time."
