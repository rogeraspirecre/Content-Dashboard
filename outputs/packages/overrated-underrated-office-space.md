## FLAG BEFORE POSTING
He calls **a brand new building OVERRATED**: "you're paying a huge premium versus buildings that aren't brand new, but also aren't that old, that are basically the same." Sierra Vista and Magnolia are new construction. A prospect who sees both could read it as an argument against paying new-construction pricing.

It's a narrower conflict than the own-vs-lease one, because his point is about paying a premium for new *space you occupy short term*, while a condo buyer is holding for years with lower maintenance and capex. Three options: cut the item, get a one-line pickup that makes the distinction ("if you're leasing for three years, new isn't worth the premium. If you're buying and holding, it is"), or post as is. The pickup is cheap and makes the verdict sharper.
