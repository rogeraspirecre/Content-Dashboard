## YouTube description
Business partnerships are marriages, and you should treat them that way before you enter into one. That's true whether it's two partners going in to run a deal together, investing money in a deal as a passive limited partner, or bringing on limited partners to fund deals you're going to run.

You have to be with people you'll be on the same page with, whose values align with yours, and who all want things to go great. How good your partners are may not matter much when things do go great. But things often don't go great, and when times get tough, people's true colors come through.

If you build partnerships with people where everything crumbles the moment things aren't going well, you'd have been better off not starting in the first place.
