## EDIT NOTES
- **Reorder the clip.** He builds to the best line. For short form, lead with "excited or terrified," then let the reasoning play. The current order buries the payoff at 30 seconds.
- **The binary is what makes it work.** "Very high or essentially zero" is a real position, not encouragement. Don't trim it down to the optimistic half.
- **Same lane as the skill clip and the regret clip,** and all three now have him talking about commitment through hardship. Three in a row will feel repetitive. Spread them across weeks, or make them a deliberate three-part series with different on-screen labels.
- **Recruiting use.** This is the clearest "should you do this job" answer he's given. Worth using in hiring: send it to broker candidates before an interview and it screens people for you.
- **Reach note:** it's about brokers specifically, which narrows it, but the argument works for any commission career. If you want the wider cut, have him film a version that says "commission business" instead of "broker."
