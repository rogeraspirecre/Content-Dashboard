## YouTube description
In this video, I answer what you should actually be doing to advertise a commercial vacancy. It isn't a matter of picking the one thing that works. You have to do all of it.

It starts with great marketing materials and making the property look attractive on a screen. Good photos, real branding. Then you get those materials in front of people. The listing platforms, with CoStar listed correctly. The property on your own website, with SEO and enough traffic that it actually surfaces when people search. A sign in the yard for drive-by calls. Content created about the property. Paid ads running on it.

Then the part people forget: when leads come in, respond fast. Know the answers to their questions. Know the property. Sell them on it. Get them what they need quickly, whether that's proposals, space plans, construction pricing, or all three.

You have to do a lot of things right to lease commercial space. Any one of them missing reduces your odds and adds time to the vacancy.
