## YouTube description
How do you know when a real estate market is overbuilt? The telltale sign is when new supply being delivered is greater than net absorption, meaning more new space is hitting the market than tenants are actually taking up. One or two quarters of that can happen. When you see it quarter after quarter, you have a big problem.

The harder part is that by the time you find out a market is overbuilt, it can already be too late. Finding land, buying it, getting permits, building, and getting the building ready to sell or lease takes one to three years. The market can change a lot in that window. It might not have been oversupplied when you bought the land or broke ground, and then all of a sudden it is.

That's why development is such a risky game. Developers make a lot of money when things go well, but a lot of things can also go wrong.
