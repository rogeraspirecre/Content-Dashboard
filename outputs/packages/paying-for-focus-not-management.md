## YouTube description
In this video, I talk about a sign it's time to replace your property manager: you're constantly having to manage them.

Think about the purpose of a property manager. Obviously it's to manage and take care of the property. But more than that, it's so you can focus on your core business. Your core business isn't property management. That's the whole reason you hired someone.

So if your manager isn't giving you that, you're paying money to be free to focus on your business, and you're still not focusing on your business. That cost is much higher than whatever you're paying in management fees.

Make sure you're not throwing good money away, and make sure you have someone who adds value instead of deteriorating it. Bad management is how properties fall apart fast.
