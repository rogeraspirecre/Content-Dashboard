## The argument
- Yes, he has talked clients out of deals.
- A broker's number one responsibility is the client's best interest.
- If he thinks the client shouldn't do the deal, he says so.
- That goes against his own interest, since he doesn't get paid if the deal dies.
- Doing what's best for the client at your own expense always pays off in the long run.

## YouTube description
Have I ever talked a client out of a deal? Absolutely.

As a broker, your number one responsibility is to do what's in the best interest of your client. If a client shouldn't do a deal, or if I think they shouldn't, I'm going to tell them. That goes against my own interest, because if the deal doesn't happen, I don't get paid.

But doing what's best for your client, even when it costs you, always pays off in the long run.
