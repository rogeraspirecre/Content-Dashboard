## YouTube description
In this video, I rank the ways to get your first client, from S tier to F.

Cold calling is S. There's no better way. You control the inputs, which is how many calls you make. You can follow up, be persistent, and get in front of people.

LinkedIn DMs land high too, because it's just another form of cold outreach. There's a lot of spam on LinkedIn, but there's spam everywhere. You work through it.

Posting content gets a C as a first-client strategy, even though over time it becomes S. It's hard to get people to watch your content when you have no credibility yet.

Referrals get a D. How are you going to get referred your first client when you haven't done business with anyone? Referrals are for the experienced.

Networking events get a D. It can happen, but if that's your main strategy you'll be waiting a long time.

Asking a family member gets an F. If you have a family member who can be a client, great, but that's not within your control, so what's the point of building a plan around it?
