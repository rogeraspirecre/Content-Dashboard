## YouTube description
If you want to grow, you have to delegate. And if you want to delegate, you have to commit to no longer doing it yourself.

The process is simple. You show someone how to do it. Then you do it with them. Then they do it and you review. It takes longer to teach someone else than it would take to do it yourself, which is why most people never get through it. But once you go through that process and they get good at it, you never have to do it again, and often they end up better at it than you were.

It's like investing in a real estate deal or a stock. You put the money out there and it comes back to you later. Same thing here. You spend more time now so you get your time back later.
