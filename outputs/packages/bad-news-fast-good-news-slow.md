## YouTube description
Communication is what makes or breaks relationships over the long run. When there's bad news, it's natural to want to deliver it slowly. When there's good news, it's natural to want to share it fast. To build and keep relationships for the long term, you need to do the exact opposite.

Bad news needs to travel at lightning speed. You're going to have to deliver it eventually, so holding onto it only makes it worse. Good news should wait until you have all the facts and you know it's actually going to happen. If you get excited, speak too soon, and it doesn't work out, you've let the client down.

Bad news travels fast. Good news travels slow. That's how you build relationships that last.
