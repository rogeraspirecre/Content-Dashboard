## YouTube description
In this video, I break down the two ways to market commercial real estate.

The first is reactive marketing, which is what almost everybody does. Put up a sign, put up a listing, and hope the phone rings.

The second is proactive marketing. That's actively reaching out to and getting in front of people who might be interested in your building.

If you want to market a property effectively, you need both. And the best way to be proactive right now is social media and paid advertising. You create content about the building, make it interesting to your target customers, and then put it in front of those specific audiences.

If you're only doing reactive marketing in 2026, that isn't good enough anymore.
