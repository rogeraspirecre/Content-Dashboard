## YouTube description
It's crazy when people say real estate always goes up in value. Here are the numbers on a property we used to manage that show how false that is.

In 2012, the operating expenses on this property were $7.47 a foot. In 2024, they were $12.81 a foot. That's a 71% increase in expenses.

Now the revenue side. A tenant signed a lease in 2012 at $16.50 a square foot gross, meaning that's all they pay with no expenses on top. In 2024, that same tenant renewed at $16.00 a foot. So over twelve years, expenses went up 71% and the rent went down 3%.

Real estate sells on a multiple of the income it produces. When rents are flat or down while expenses are up, it destroys the value. In 2012 the net operating income was $722,000. At an 8% cap rate, the building was worth about $9 million. In 2024 the NOI was $255,000. At the same 8% cap rate, it's worth $3.2 million.

That building lost $5.8 million in value over twelve years. Next time someone tells you real estate always goes up, tell them that story.
