- **Verify the first answer.** The transcript reads "a good broker always follows" — almost certainly "follows up," but confirm before it goes on a graphic.
- **This is the insider lane.** Brokers, landlords, tenants, vacancy. Per Finding 7, sets built on items only industry people recognize land in the 2-3K range, not the 200K+ range. Nothing wrong with that — this is rapport content and it's good rapport content. Just don't judge it against the ranking videos.
- **One line to reuse.** "Vacancy usually means you have a marketing problem" is the same argument as the reactive-vs-proactive clip and the vacancies checklist. Three pieces now make that case. That's the strongest owner-facing position in the library and it's worth a standalone video, not just a one-liner.
