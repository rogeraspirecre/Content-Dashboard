## The verdicts
- **Restaurant: 9.** Plenty of space out there, but so many things have to go right for a restaurant to succeed. Very hard to find the right space for that specific tenant.
- **Fitness studio: 8.** Hard to go in an office building because of HVAC challenges and lack of exposure. Retail is often too expensive, or they can't afford the build out. Tough business.
- **Medical practice: 4.** Not hard to find space, but be ready to pay a lot for the build out. Medical build outs are crazy expensive.
- **Law firm: 3.** Nothing complex about it. They want a prestigious address, great views, and a building with amenities so they can convince lawyers to stay till midnight billing hours.
- **Auto repair shop: 9.** The vast majority of landlords won't even consider you. One of the harder businesses to get an owner to accept.
- **Daycare: 3.** Visibility isn't a big issue, proximity to the client base matters more. Good daycare operators find space pretty easily at scale. There are a lot of not-good operators out there.
- **Small wealth management firm: 2.** Plenty of options, and landlords like having them in their building.

## EDIT NOTES
- **Label the scale.** In Rate It Out of Ten, ten was good. Here ten is hardest. Without "10 = hardest" on screen, viewers will read "wealth management 2" as a bad grade. Put it up in the first two seconds and keep it in the corner.
- **Reorder:** auto repair, restaurant, fitness studio, medical, law firm, daycare, wealth management. Hardest to easiest opens on the surprise and gives the list a clean shape.
- **Best line is the law firm one.** "So they can convince those lawyers to be there till midnight billing hours" is the only joke in the clip. Don't trim it for time.
- **Transcript fixes:** "in their ruling" is "in their building," and "they or they can't afford" is a stumble. Clean both in captions.
- **This is top-of-funnel.** Restaurants, gyms, daycares, auto shops, law firms are all recognizable to people who know nothing about commercial real estate (Finding 7). This should outperform the owner-education clips. Treat it as a reach piece and give it a real thumbnail.
- **Obvious Pt. 2.** Dentist, coffee shop, barber, nail salon, church, brewery, car wash, urgent care, tattoo shop, smoke shop, daycare franchise. Same format, more recognizable businesses. Add smash room for the iSmash tie-in.
- **Ad angle.** Auto repair, fitness, and restaurants being hard to place is a tenant-rep pitch: those owners now know they need a broker. Worth one ad test aimed at hard-to-place tenants.
