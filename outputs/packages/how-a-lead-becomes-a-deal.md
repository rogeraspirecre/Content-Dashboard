## YouTube description
We built this in HubSpot through a lot of trial and error. From the moment a lead comes in to the moment we close the deal, there are automations running through the whole process.

Somebody reaches out about one building or three buildings. The agent just has to copy a certain email address, and the automations fire to create the leads. If you're a tenant rep broker and you reach out on two buildings, that's two leads. The system creates both, ties each one to the right listing in the CRM, and now you can follow up knowing exactly who asked about which building.

Then, with a custom solution we built using AI, you can export a leasing report or an activity report at the click of a button, with every lead separated by the stage it's in. It starts as a lead. Once they tour, it becomes a deal. Then a proposal, lease out, closed. The report updates as the deal moves, so you can generate it any time.
