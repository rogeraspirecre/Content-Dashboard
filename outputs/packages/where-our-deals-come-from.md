## YouTube description
In this video, I answer a fair question from the comments. Someone said my point about marketing was vague and asked what I actually meant.

At Aspire CRE we close over 20 deals a month. When we look at where those closed deals came from, they come from all over. Some from listing platforms. Some from our website. Some from ads. Some from social media content. Some from referrals. Some from people we already know. Some from sign calls.

There are that many different ways a tenant or buyer finds a commercial property. So if you list a property online and expect things to happen, that's not a strategy, and your space will probably sit for a long time.

Marketing commercial property in 2026 is an all of the above approach. Not a few photos and a description on a website.
