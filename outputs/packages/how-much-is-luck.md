## YouTube description
How much of the commercial real estate business is luck? It always looks like luck when somebody is successful. And if you really drill down into it, of course they were lucky at times along the way. But that's not why they succeeded. They succeeded because they put themselves in a position to get lucky. Luck is not going to find you if you don't put yourself in a position to get lucky.

What do I mean by that? Make the calls. Get in front of people. Show up every day. Work hard.

This is a simple business, but it's not easy. What I mean is that it's very easy to know what you need to do to be successful. The only question is whether you're willing to show up every day and do those things long enough, without letting distractions or failure get you down and make you quit.
