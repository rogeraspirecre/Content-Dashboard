## The answers
- New client: "We send updates in real time, plus a full activity report every two weeks. Does every two weeks work for you?" (sets the standard, then asks for buy-in)
- Tenant looking for space: "What are the top things most important for you to have in your space?"
- Owner with a vacancy: "Why do you think it hasn't leased yet?"
- Broker you might hire: "What do you do to make sure your clients have the best experience working with you?"
- Property manager: "Do you understand the accounting side of property management?"

## YouTube description
In this video, I go rapid fire on the first question I ask in different situations.

A new client: I tell them we send updates in real time, plus a full activity report every two weeks detailing everything happening at the property, and then I ask whether every two weeks works for them.

A tenant looking for space: what are the top things that are most important for you to have?

An owner with a vacancy: why do you think it hasn't leased yet?

A broker I might hire: what do you do to make sure your clients have the best experience working with you?

A property manager: do you understand the accounting side of property management?
