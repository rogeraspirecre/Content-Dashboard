## YouTube description
There are a couple of different categories of automation at our firm. The first is the simple stuff.

We use Monday for project management. So if an agent closes a deal and needs an invoice, they fill out a form, and a bunch of automations fire to notify the different people involved in making that invoice what they need to do. When they finish, it goes back to the agent for review.

If they need a listing agreement, a flyer, a pitch, a floor plan updated because a space became available, or a space taken down because it leased, it all goes into the same system. It's very simple, and everybody knows what to do.
