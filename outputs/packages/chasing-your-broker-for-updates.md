## YouTube description
The most frustrating thing commercial real estate owners deal with, and shouldn't have to, is chasing down updates on their own property.

On the marketing side, it's a broker who isn't sending updates or activity reports, so you have to call them to find out what's going on. Are they actively marketing your property, or just sitting around hoping the phone rings? On the property management side, it's chasing the manager to confirm the work got done, following up on monthly reports that didn't show up on time, and checking whether the preventive maintenance in the budget is actually happening.

The reality is simple. If you have to constantly chase your broker or your property manager for updates, they're probably not doing a very good job.
