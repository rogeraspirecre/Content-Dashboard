## YouTube description
In this video, I respond to a comment saying I'm not a fan of side hustles because I'm not a hustler, and that money can be made doing pretty much anything.

He's right about the second part. Money can be made doing almost anything. But you have to be genuinely good at what you do to make it. If you're not good and you expect money anyway, you're hoping to get lucky. Hoping for luck isn't a strategy, and it usually ends in failure.

That's my actual issue with side hustles. Doing three things part time instead of committing to one thing all in makes it much harder to build the skills, experience, and expertise that let you make real money at anything.
