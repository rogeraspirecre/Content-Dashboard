## YouTube description
A lot of tenants think hiring a broker to represent them on a commercial lease is pointless. Here's why it isn't.

First, the landlord is in the real estate business. If you're not, you're at a huge disadvantage from the start, negotiating against someone who has done hundreds or thousands of deals. Second, your time is your most valuable asset as a business owner, and it should go toward growing your business, not learning the nuances of a space search.

Third, you don't know the market. You can research, you can ask AI, you can look at LoopNet or Crexi. But there's so much nuance in what makes a deal good that you're not going to find it on your own. You need someone who has done hundreds of deals in your specific market to know whether your terms are actually market.

Fourth, you don't know what you don't know. These leases are 30, 40, 50 pages. There's far more in them than the rental rate: operating expense provisions, insurance, indemnification, and much more.

And on top of all that, when you hire a broker, the landlord pays the fee. You get the expertise with no money out of your pocket. If you don't use a broker, you don't save that money. The landlord already assumes they're paying a broker on every deal, so they just pay their own broker more. You did all the work yourself, you probably got a worse deal, and you saved nothing.
