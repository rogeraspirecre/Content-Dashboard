## The argument
- Tenants renew when they're happy. Period.
- Tenants don't want to move. Moving is a pain, disrupts the business, and costs money. If they're happy, why leave?
- What makes them happy: responsive management, a landlord who acts like a partner, reinvestment in the property, keeping it somewhere employees and clients want to come, and responsiveness to their needs.
- Thinking ahead and being proactive instead of waiting for bad things and being slow to fix them.
- Don't do things that make your tenants want to shop around.
