## YouTube description
Where do you actually find good industrial deals? There are two ways to approach it.

The first is doing it yourself. You can look on LoopNet, CoStar, Crexi, and you can look up owners of properties that fit your criteria and call them directly. It works, but it's slow, and you're seeing what everyone else sees.

The second is working with a broker who specializes in industrial. A good broker has a pipeline of off-market deals, learns exactly what you're looking for - down to the deal size and the tenant profile - and sends you deals they've already vetted instead of letting you spin your wheels on the internet.
