## YouTube description
First, you've got to expect pain and suffering. This is a difficult business, not an easy one, and if you're not willing to go through tough times, you're not going to make it. Real estate is cyclical. It goes up and it goes down, and there are good times, bad times, easy times and hard times. If you expect it to always be easy and up and to the right, you won't last.

Second, do not get into too much debt. The number one way people get crushed in this business is too much debt. They make overly optimistic assumptions, and when the market is good they assume it's only going to keep getting better. That's what causes the major problems.

Third, this is a relationship business and your reputation is everything. It takes your entire career to build a reputation and five seconds to destroy it.

Real estate is a great business if you're willing to have a long term mindset, stick it out through the tough times, do the right thing, and stay with the plan no matter what's going on around you.
