# Stop Asking Your Boss What to Do
Format: talking head | Lane: career / aspirational (BROAD crossover) | Status: filmed
Nothing about this requires real estate. Cut the RE framing entirely and it plays as career advice for anyone in an entry-level job — that's how it travels.

## The technique
Don't ask "what should we do?" Instead:
1. Pretend the decision is yours
2. Ask what you'd think about and what you'd need to see
3. Go to them with "here's the situation, here's what I think, here's why"
4. Get corrected — that's the education
Result: you learn to make decisions instead of learning to move paperwork.

## YouTube description
In this video, I talk about where confidence actually comes from, and how to build it before you have the experience to justify it.

You've seen people who've owned property for a long time make hard decisions and take bad news calmly, completely confident they can fix it. That comes from experience. But you're never going to wake up one day and decide you now have enough experience to act with confidence. You have to start acting confident before you feel ready.

Here's the practical part. Believe you're intelligent enough to make judgment calls from the data and facts in front of you. Then go to people above you and test your thinking.

But don't go to them asking what should we do. If you do that and watch them decide, you'll never learn. Instead, pretend you're the one who has to make the call. What would you think about? What would you need to see? Then go to them and say, here's the situation, here's what I think, and here's why.

They'll tell you why that's not the move, and you'll learn how decisions actually get made instead of learning how to move paperwork back and forth.
