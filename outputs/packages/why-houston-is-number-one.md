## YouTube description
In this video, I answer a genuine question from the comments: what makes Houston the undisputed number one city for business?

Houston is an entrepreneur's paradise, and it comes down to the people. It's an incredibly diverse city full of people who came here from all over the world to build a life and chase the American dream. They don't care where you came from. They don't care where you went to school. If you work hard, provide a service, and add value to them, they'll give you a shot.

A lot of other cities aren't like that. It's who's your family, where did you grow up, and if you don't have the right answers, you may not get a chance at all.

The cream rises to the top in Houston. Anybody with a dream who's willing to work and stick with it long term can do huge things here.
