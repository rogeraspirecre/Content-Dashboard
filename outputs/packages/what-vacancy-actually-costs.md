## YouTube description
The funny thing about vacancy is that most owners view it as lost income. It's much worse than that.

Most commercial properties are triple net, which means the tenants pay the operating expenses. But when you have a vacancy, the owner eats the expenses on that vacant space. If a property is 75% leased, the tenants are paying the property tax bill, the insurance bill, and the maintenance bill, but they're only paying 75% of those costs. The other 25% is paid by the landlord.

So treat your vacancy that way. You're losing income, and you're also paying expenses you wouldn't be paying if the property were leased.
