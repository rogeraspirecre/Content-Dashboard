## The verdicts
- **Trades: couldn't be more safe.** "I love Elon. Guy's incredible. But Optimus is not going to be fixing your toilet anytime soon."
- **Sales: safe.** AI handles the basic inbound, which is the stuff good salespeople weren't doing anyway. You still have to sell human to human.
- **Accountants: safe.** "I wish more than anybody that I could just have AI do all of our accounting." Too much nuance. Safe "at great cost, expense and headache to me."
- **Software developers: completely safe,** with a condition. A developer who doesn't learn to use AI is not safe. The idea that companies fire everyone, go to the beach, and the software builds and maintains and sells itself is crazy.
- **Graphic design: split.** Good designers are safe and more important than ever, because their output went way up. Bad designers are "completely done, toasted, will never have a job again unless they get good." Order takers templating things have tough times ahead.
- **Data entry: not safe.** AI takes it. But a curious data entry person can translate those skills, and attention to detail matters more than ever, because somebody has to make sure AI isn't going crazy.
- **Teachers: very safe.** Hopefully they make more as AI makes the economy more efficient. "I do not want AI teaching my kids."
- **Therapists: not going anywhere.** "Does ChatGPT really know what they're talking about? Does your therapist really know what they're talking about? I don't know either way."
- **Lawyers: safe, with exceptions.** Nobody likes lawyers, nobody wants to talk to them or pay them, but you need one for certain things. Some areas won't need one anymore.

## EDIT NOTES

- **Cold open on the Optimus line.** Named person, a joke, and a verdict in one sentence.
