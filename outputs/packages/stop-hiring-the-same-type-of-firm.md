## YouTube description
In this video, I talk about why brokers and property managers get a bad rap, and what to do about it.

A lot of owners have bad experiences and decide to handle their own property management or brokerage work. The problem is that if you're not in those businesses, it's very hard to do them well. You may think you're doing fine, but you don't know what you don't know. Unless you're doing it a lot, over a long period, and making real investments of time and money to learn it, you're doing yourself a disservice.

The better approach is to find good providers. There are good ones out there. I don't care how many you've worked with and been unhappy with.

And be accountable about your own pattern. If you've hired the same type of firm several times and it hasn't worked, try a different type. All small firms and it keeps failing? Try a bigger one. All big firms? Try a smaller one, or something in between.

Most importantly, hold them accountable. What did they say when they were trying to win your business? Hold them to it, keep the feedback loops short, and move on quickly if the pitch turns out to be smoke and mirrors.
