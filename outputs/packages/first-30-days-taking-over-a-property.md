- How you get to know it: do the boring things.

## YouTube description
The first 30 days after taking over a property are critical to managing, operating, and marketing it well. You have to understand the property deeply, and if you don't, you're going to miss things, make mistakes, and lose opportunities.

So how do you get to know a property? You do the boring things. You understand the leases and dig into the details. You go through the historical records. You learn who the vendors are and evaluate whether they should keep being vendors. You get to know the tenants and find out what they like about the property and what they don't.

That's a lot of work. Taking over a property the right way takes almost as much time in the first 30 days as it does over the entire next year. The transition is time consuming if you're going to do it right, and you have to put that time in up front. If you don't, the property is going to suffer.
