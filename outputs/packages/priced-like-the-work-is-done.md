## YouTube description
What's the biggest red flag I see on a property tour? A building with deferred maintenance everywhere - renovations needed, clearly not taken care of - priced at what it would be worth if the current owner had already done all the things you're going to have to do.

It makes no sense, and there are so many properties on the market at any given time where this is the case. The seller wants the buyer to pay the price the property would be worth if all the work were already done. Why would a buyer do that? They're going to pay what it's worth today, because they're the ones doing the work and taking the risk to get it to the number the seller wants now.

And the seller says, "the rents could be so much higher - you just do this, this, and this. You could add so much value." Okay. Then price it where it makes sense for someone to do those things. You can't sell it for the price I'd get after I take all the risk and do all the work.
