## CUT 1 — "The boring automation that runs a brokerage" (~40 sec)

There's a couple different categories of automation. There's simple stuff. We use monday.com for our project management. So if an agent closes a deal and needs an invoice, they fill out a form, and then a bunch of automations fire to notify the different people involved in making an invoice what they need to do, and then when they do it, it goes back to the agent for review. If they need a listing agreement, if they need a flyer, if they need a pitch, if they need a new floor plan updated because a space became available, or they leased a space that needs it taken down, it all goes into this system and it's very simple and everybody knows what to do.

## CUT 2 — "How we used to do this" (~45 sec)

When I got into the business, for the first several years of my career, we were very heavy on the listing side. Landlord rep, multi-tenant buildings. So lots of inbound leads all the time, and you have to send them information quickly, you have to know who to follow up with, and you have to report to your clients. And so it used to be you load all the leads in Excel, and then you have leasing reports in Excel for each listing. And so every time you have a call with a client, you go to the activity log in Excel, and then you copy over the leads to these leasing reports. It was incredibly inefficient. Then we started using a tool designed for landlord rep listing, but then there was no customer relationship management aspect of it. The leads go in there, you can create a report, you can do nothing else with it.

## CUT 3 — "How a lead moves through our CRM" (~45 sec)

We use HubSpot for our CRM. And so through a lot of trial and error and building in HubSpot, the way it works is from the moment a lead comes in to the moment we close a deal, there's automations running throughout the process. So somebody reaches out, whether it's on one building or three buildings, there's an automation. The agent just has to copy a certain email address, then the automations will fire to create leads. If you reach out on a building and you're a tenant rep broker, and you reach out on two buildings, that's two leads. And so it'll create both leads, it'll tie it to the listing in the CRM, and then you'll be able to follow up on those leads. You'll know who reached out on which building.
