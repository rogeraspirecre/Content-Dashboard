Job & Career Green Flag / Red Flag

What to actually look for. Brandon reacts to each signal on camera and calls it a green flag or a red flag. Open cold on the first item in each set — no intro.

Opens your browser’s print dialog — choose “Save as PDF.”

A Job Offer 01

  • Pay is way above market for the role
  • They rushed you and want an answer tomorrow
  • They can clearly explain how you’ll be measured
  • The role was “created just for you”
  • Everyone you’d work with seems checked out
  • The person who’d manage you is genuinely impressive

The Interview 02

  • They can’t tell you why the last person left
  • The owner interviews you personally
  • They ghost you two weeks, then rush
  • They ask about results, not years of experience
  • Three rounds for an entry-level job
  • They keep selling “we’re like a family”

Your Boss 03

  • Takes credit for your work
  • Still does the hardest jobs themselves
  • Only gives feedback at review time
  • Protects their people from the higher-ups
  • Changes direction every week
  • Hires people smarter than themselves

A Company Worth Working For 04

  • Growing and hiring
  • Constant reorgs and layoffs
  • The owner reinvests instead of flashing money
  • High turnover on your future team
  • Quietly profitable for years, no hype
  • Everyone’s title is inflated

A Startup Job Offer 05

  • Big equity, below-market salary
  • They can’t explain how they make money
  • The founder has built something before
  • “Pre-revenue but about to blow up”
  • You’d be employee number five
  • Less than a year of runway

Your Career Path 06

  • Same title for four years
  • You’re the least experienced person in the room
  • Your skills would transfer anywhere
  • Your entire income depends on one company
  • You’re bored but comfortable
  • You’re learning faster than you’re earning

A Remote Job 07

  • Fully remote, US, at top dollar
  • Clear outcomes, not hours watched
  • “We’ll figure out your role as we go”
  • The whole team has never met in person
  • They measure what you produce
  • Nonstop “quick calls” all day

Your Industry 08

  • AI can already do the entry-level version
  • People will always need it done in person
  • It’s consolidating into a few big players
  • High demand, not enough skilled people
  • It’s “hot” and everyone’s rushing in
  • Nobody young wants to do it anymore
Eight sets, six signals each. The insider read: an employer telling you what actually matters — which is exactly why viewers grade their own job while they watch. Judge each on views; the format does the sharing for you.
