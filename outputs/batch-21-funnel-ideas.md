# Batch 21 - 100 Funnel Ideas (2026-10-27)
60 top of funnel + 40 middle of funnel questions. Checked against posted library and backlog at title level; franchise ranking and McDonald's-real-estate dropped as existing. Research sources: bigvu creator decoder (10 realtor accounts), opus.pro 2026 short-form trends, luxurypresence RE video guide, clutch.co funnel framework, shootsta MOF formats.

## Research takeaways (formats not yet used)
1. Completion rate is the ranked metric on IG; sub-10s clips overperform. No <=10s content exists yet - see ideas 46-55.
2. POV walk-and-point clips travel without talking-head polish (red flag tour = CRE version of before/after staging).
3. Reaction format ("CPA reacts") is the most proven finance-creator format Brandon hasn't used.
4. Series with open loops drive follows - own data proves it (Pt 2 ranking 5.86M). Deal diary = serialized version.
5. MOF = demo/comparison/case-story: process questions answered with real numbers.

### Green flag / red flag (items built to go either way)
26. Landlord Green Flag or Red Flag - answers same day but raises rent every year; owns 40 buildings; self-manages; 2 months free offered; requires personal guarantee; always one vacancy.
27. Business Partner Green Flag or Red Flag - more money less time; everything in writing day one; last partnership ended badly; best friend; much richer than you; insists on 50/50.
28. Job Offer Green Flag or Red Flag - unlimited PTO; two-day interview process; counteroffer when you quit; "we're a family"; equity instead of salary; boss promoted twice in 4 years.
29. First Employee Green Flag or Red Flag - asks about overtime in interview; negotiates hard; Fortune 500 background; overqualified; friend's kid; asks for equity.
30. Deal Green Flag or Red Flag - seller in a hurry; listed 200 days; off-market; seller financing; tenant signed 10-year lease last month; shoebox financials.
31. Contractor Green Flag or Red Flag - cheapest bid by a mile; booked 3 months out; 50% upfront; brand-new truck; no website all referrals; lawyer-drafted contract.
32. Customer Green Flag or Red Flag - negotiates every invoice but pays same day; huge first order; "money is no object"; wants exclusivity; best referrer is smallest account.
33. Lender Green Flag or Red Flag - pre-approval in an hour; lowest rate most fees; local bank knows your name; calls quarterly; personal guarantee on everything; life insurance assignment.

## Middle of funnel (61-100) - questions answered on camera, 30-60s, one real number each

### Buying your building (owner-occupiers)
61. Should I buy or lease my building? - the 3 deciding numbers; when leasing wins anyway; the SBA 51% rule.
62. How much do I actually need down? - SBA 10% vs conventional 25%; what lenders weigh.
63. What does my business need to show a lender? - two years of returns, DSCR plain-English, personal-guarantee reality.
64. How do I know what a building is actually worth? - why the listing price isn't it.
65. What's an LOI and why does it come before the contract?
66. How long does buying a building actually take? - the real timeline and where it stalls.
67. Which inspections matter? - roof, HVAC, Phase 1, survey, ADA.
68. What's a Phase 1 and why does my lender require it?
69. What happens at closing? - who's in the room, what you sign, when you get keys.
70. Should my business own the building or should I own it and lease it to my business? (CPA caveat)

### Leasing (tenant side)
71. What does $24/SF NNN actually cost per month? - full math, real 2,000 SF example.
72. What can I negotiate besides rent? - TI, free rent, NNN caps, renewal options, exclusivity.
73. How much free rent should I ask for? - norms by lease term.
74. What's a TI allowance and who really pays for it?
75. What if I need out of my lease early? - sublease vs assignment vs buyout, real costs.
76. When should I start looking before my lease expires? - why waiting kills leverage.
77. Why do landlords care what business I'm in? - use clauses, exclusives, parking math.
78. Who pays my broker when I lease space? - the landlord does; how that works, what to ask.
79. Gross vs modified gross vs NNN - one space run three ways.
80. Why the cheapest space per foot can be the most expensive deal - load factor, buildout, NNN surprises.

### Investing
81. How do I buy my first commercial property with under $500K? - what that buys in Houston.
82. What's a good cap rate in Houston right now? - by product type; why the answer is "for what?"
83. Due diligence on the tenants, not just the building - estoppels, financials, remaining term.
84. How do I value a building with a vacant unit? - how sellers hide the vacancy cost.
85. What makes a tenant "credit" and why does it change the price? - Starbucks vs local coffee shop, same corner.
86. Single-tenant or multi-tenant for my first deal?
87. What kills more first deals: the building or the financing? - rate resets, the refi trap.
88. How do balloon payments work? - 5-year term, 25-year amortization, the year-5 problem.
89. What happens when my loan resets in a high-rate year? - the math that forces sales.
90. Cash or debt if I have the money? - leverage both directions, the sleep factor.

### Process & working with a broker
91. What does a buyer's broker actually do all day? - pipeline, underwriting, negotiation, vendor quarterback.
92. How do off-market deals actually happen? - who gets the call, how you get on the list. (Follow-up to the LoopNet clip.)
93. What should I have ready before I call a broker? - the 4 answers that make you a priority.
94. How do brokers get paid on a purchase and what does it cost me?
95. Why did my offer lose when it was highest? - terms beat price: certainty, timeline, earnest money.
96. What's earnest money and can I lose it? - the option-period calendar.
97. How many properties should I tour before buying? - data answer vs patience answer.
98. What questions should I ask on a property tour? - the 5 sellers won't volunteer. (Pairs with Red Flag Tour.)
99. When is the right time to sell? - loan maturity, cap rates, your next use for the money.
100. What will my building be worth in 10 years? - why nobody knows; the 3 things that decide it.
