Source: flyer (Nov 2025). Property: 25819 Cypresswood Drive, Spring TX 77373. **Sales team: Bradley Segreto, Benjamin Nicknish, Luke Stavinoha — NOT Brandon.** Brandon can still front the creative as the face of the firm, but the CTA routes to this team.

## Product facts
- **1,125 SF units** (much smaller than Magnolia's 1,512-4,536 SF — different, smaller buyer)
- 2025 construction, new
- Flyer says "Units Available Q4 2025" — **stale as of Sep 2026, confirm current availability and delivery before spend**
- At the entrance of Breckenridge Park, a master-planned neighborhood community
- Cypresswood Drive frontage and visibility; flyer explicitly targets neighborhood-oriented tenants
- Direct access to the Hardy Toll Road
- Amenities within the trade area: Starbucks, Chipotle, Panera, Chick-fil-A, Whataburger, Walmart Supercenter, Kohl's, Michaels, Ross, Crumbl, Popeyes, Rudy's, plus dozens more
- Demographics: 1 mile — 11,730 people, median HH income $99,545, average HH income $134,444, median age 41.5. 3 miles — 103,726 people, AHHI $97,550. 5 miles — 276,199 people.
- MISSING before launch: price per unit, HOA/CAM, current delivery date, parking ratio, whether units can be combined, whether medical use is permitted

## Who this is for (1,125 SF changes the audience)
This is the **first office** size, not the established-practice size. Primary: solo and small professional operators who are working from home or subleasing a room, 2 to 8 people. Insurance agents, CPAs and bookkeepers, attorneys (solo/small), title and mortgage, financial advisors, therapists and counselors, chiropractors, med spa and aesthetics, IT and managed services, staffing, tutoring and test prep, and the back office for home-service businesses (HVAC, roofing, plumbing, pest, landscaping) whose crews work in the field.
Secondary: investors buying one or two units to lease to that same profile.
Geography: Spring, Klein, Old Town Spring, Tomball, Humble, and the south edge of The Woodlands.

## Angles, strongest first
1. **Your first office.** The 1,125 SF story: you've outgrown the house, and the next step is ownership, not a lease.
2. **Stop paying rent, build equity.** Core condo angle, proven on Magnolia and Sierra Vista.
3. **The customers are already here.** 103,726 people within three miles, $134,444 average household income within one mile. For anyone whose clients come to them, that's the whole pitch.
4. **Frontage and signage.** Cypresswood Drive visibility and your name on the building, at the entrance to a neighborhood of your future clients.
5. **Stop the commute.** Direct Hardy Toll access, work where you live instead of driving into town.
6. **Brand new, nobody else's build-out.** 2025 construction, first owner, no inherited layout to undo.
7. **Small enough to own.** The entry-level ownership angle: this is the smallest check in the office condo market, which makes ownership possible for a one to five person business.
8. **The neighborhood-serving business.** At the entrance of a master-planned community, which means the customer base walks and drives past you daily.
9. **Investor:** small unit, small check, lease it to the professionals moving into this corridor.
10. **Scarcity and timing** (only once current availability is confirmed).
