# Ad Brief: Sierra Vista Office Condos

Source: June brochure (Downloads). Property: new-construction office condos, Meridiana Parkway, Iowa Colony TX 77583 (288 corridor). Construction begins Q4 2026. Developer partner: Malabar Hill Capital. Sales: Brandon Avedikian (founder), William Harris, Jared Starnes.

## Product facts
- 1,120 SF units: private offices, conference room, kitchenette, restroom
- Move-in ready, fully built out
- Early bird pricing from $320,000, first 6 units only
- Condo association handles roof, exterior, grounds, lot, trash; CAM ~$250/mo
- Meridiana Parkway visibility, dual access
- Committed neighbors on the corner: Learning Care Group, Jiffy Lube, O'Reilly, Shipley Do-Nuts, Snap Fitness; retail + residential development adjacent
- Near Meridiana, one of the fastest-growing master-planned communities in Texas

## Target audience (run as two ad sets)
1. **Owner-occupiers (primary):** service professionals living within ~15 min (Iowa Colony, Manvel, Pearland, Sierra Vista, Sterling Lakes, Alvin). CPAs, insurance/financial advisors, attorneys, therapists, medical/dental satellites, title/mortgage, engineers, real estate teams. Currently renting, commuting up 288, or working from home. Age ~32–60.
2. **Small investors (secondary):** local buyers wanting a $320K entry into CRE in a growth corridor, leasing to profile 1.

## Pain points / desires (ordered by ad-copy punch)
1. Stop renting your office; rent builds the landlord's equity
2. Professional space 5 minutes from home; no 288 commute; no coffee-shop client meetings
3. Ownership without the landlord job (association handles maintenance; ~$250/mo CAM)
4. Get in before the growth is priced in (pre-construction pricing, national tenants already committed, thousands of new rooftops)
5. Zero build-out hassle + parkway visibility/signage
