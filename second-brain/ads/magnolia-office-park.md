Source: March 2026 flyer. Property: single-story office condos, 31300-31368 Nichols Sawmill Rd, Magnolia TX 77355. Sales: Brandon Avedikian (713) 347-2904, Emily Avedikian, Matthew Trow.

## Audiences (two ad sets)
1. **Owner-users (primary):** medical first (doctors, dentists, therapists, chiro, vets) + CPAs, attorneys, advisors within ~20 min (Magnolia, Tomball, Pinehurst, NW Houston fringe). Renting or home-based.
2. **Investors:** buy and lease to profile 1; growth-corridor thesis.

## Hook formula (Roger, Jul 2026 — applies to ALL property ads)
Direct callouts that name the person with money, then hit what they're already thinking.
- **By size:** "If you're a business owner with 5-10 employees" (PROVEN performer)
- **By area:** "Business owners in Magnolia!"
- **By business type:** doctors, dentists, CPAs, attorneys (medical first where use allows)
- **By pain/desire:** tired of paying rent, building equity, lease renewal coming
- **Stacked = strongest:** callout + pain in one line ("Magnolia business owners with 5-10 employees: you're one signature away from never paying rent again.")
