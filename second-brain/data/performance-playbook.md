# Performance Playbook

Data-driven findings from 219 Instagram reels, Mar 24 – Jul 6, 2026 (`data/content-database.csv`). Update this file whenever new data comes in.

## Headline numbers
- Median views: ~11,900. Mean: ~199,000 (heavily skewed by 9 videos over 1M).
- Top video: "Which Jobs Actually Make You a Millionaire?" — 8.1M views.
- p75 ≈ 43K, p90 ≈ 269K. Anything over ~50K is a real winner; over 250K is top-decile.

## Finding 1: The "Ranking" format is the engine (10x gap)
| Category | n | Median views |
|---|---|---|
| Ranking / tier-list format | 76 | 52,285 |
| Broad money/career topics | 25 | 25,550 |
| Other | 26 | 9,792 |
| Niche CRE (brokers, vacancy, tenants, PM) | 92 | 4,901 |

Every video over 1M views is a rapid-fire ranking on a **broad** topic: jobs that make millionaires, ways to make money, college degrees by ROI, jobs AI will replace, rich vs middle-class vs poor habits, work-life balance.

## Finding 2: The winning hook pattern
Every viral video opens **mid-list, no intro, no setup** — first word is an item, second beat is the verdict, third is a hot take:
> "Doctor. 10, you're gonna be a millionaire, but the path to get there is brutal…"
> "Investing in crypto. That's how the middle class becomes poor."

The underperformers open with slow context-setting ("In this video…" energy, or a wind-up before the point).

## Finding 3: Niche CRE content is the drag — but it's the funnel
Niche CRE topics median ~4.9K views. June was flooded with them (26 of 66 posts, median 3.2K) and the whole account's median fell. These videos serve the brokerage business, so the answer isn't "stop" — it's ratio control and format transplant: package CRE takes inside ranking/versus formats (e.g. "Ranking the Worst States to Own Real Estate" works; "Why Vacancy Gets More Dangerous the Longer It Sits" doesn't).

## Finding 4: Reposting winners works
Re-running proven videos weeks later still pulls 38K–244K ("Ranking Success Habits Pt. 2" repost: 244K). Winners have a second life; build a repost calendar from the top-decile library.

## Finding 5: Length
- Under 60s: median 11–17K. 60–90s: median 6.9K (weakest — mostly the talking-head CRE monologues). 90s+: median 24K (mostly long rankings that hold attention).
- Rule of thumb: rapid-fire list content can run long; single-idea monologues must stay tight or die.

## Finding 6: Monthly trend (watch this)

Post-April decline correlates with the content mix shifting toward niche CRE. Volume went UP while median went DOWN — more posts didn't help; mix did the damage.


## Finding 7: Real estate content travels when the ITEMS are recognizable (added Jul 13)
The niche-CRE-is-a-drag rule needs refining. RE content that pulled 100K+:

| Video | Views | Length |
|---|---|---|
| What Commercial Tenants Actually Want From Landlords | 274,782 | 41s |
| Ranking the Best and Worst Commercial Real Estate Tenants | 268,544 | 50s |
| Ranking commercial tenants | 200,000 | 50s |
| Ranking Decades of Life by Wealth-Building Potential | 192,657 | 44s |
| Best Types Of Real Estate Investments | 119,554 | 113s |

Tenant rankings work because the items are Starbucks, Chipotle, nail salons, gyms — things everyone sees in every strip center. Vacancy, property management, and broker-accountability content dies at 2-3K because there is nothing recognizable in it. **Select RE topics by item recognition, not by importance to owners.** Shorter wins here too: 41-50s pulled 200-275K; the 98s retail-tenant version pulled 29K.

**Lane hierarchy confirmed (Jul):**
1. Broad money/career rankings: 27K-272K (the engine)
2. RE-flavored rankings (recognizable items): 10-13K (RE Jobs 13.1K, Asset Classes 11K, Would You Rather RE 11K, RE Gurus 10.1K) — ~4x niche CRE, but ~1/3 of broad. Middle lane, keep at ~25% mix.
3. Green/Red Flag: 17.2K + 11.1K first two outings — mid-pack, not yet breakout.
4. Niche CRE owner/tenant education: 1.4-3.5K, fully confirmed floor. Funnel/ads only.

No 1M+ breakout in July (April had 8.2M). Median health recovered; next lever is hook quality + mechanic variety (see Batch 8 in backlog) to hunt outliers again.

## Working ratios (starting hypothesis, revise with data)
- ~50% ranking/tier-list formats on broad money/career/business topics (reach)
- ~25% CRE takes transplanted into proven formats (reach + relevance)
- ~25% pure niche CRE (funnel/authority) — accept lower views, judge on leads not reach
