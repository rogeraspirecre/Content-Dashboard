# Collab: iSmash (smash room tenant)

Aspire closed their lease. Goal: content at their site that doubles as a tenant testimonial for us and exposure for them.

## Strategy
Do NOT shoot real estate content there. Shoot the BROAD money/career content there. Rankings pull 200K-8M; niche CRE pulls 2-3K. iSmash gets real exposure only if they appear in reach content. The smash room becomes the SET for the proven ranking format, which also solves Brandon's visual problem (rankings are talking-head).

## Shoot plan (one session, ~2 hrs, 5-6 pieces)
1. **Ranking the Fastest Ways to Lose Money, smash edition** — every F-tier verdict, smash something. Original talking-head version did 451K.
2. **Green Flag / Red Flag, smash the red flags** — works with any list in this backlog. Red flag smash, green flag set down gently.
3. **Bad money advice, smashed** — each cliche destroyed as he grades it.
4. **Brandon does the smash room** — first-timer reaction, off-format, human.
5. **Tenant story (the testimonial)** — 1 min interview with owner: why this space, what they looked for, how it came together. Only piece where RE leads.
6. **"Would you lease to this business?"** — landlord POV on unusual tenants; flatters iSmash as the answer.

## What we need from them
- ~2 hours, slow period or before opening
- Owner on camera for the interview
- Access to a room or two plus items to break
- Permission to post and tag

## What they get
- Tagged/mentioned in everything posted
- All raw footage for their own channels
- Exposure to an audience that has never heard of them

Sent as Brandon. Short and direct: view counts in para 2 (that's why they say yes), two scannable lists, close asks for TIMING not interest. Optional warm-relationship line: "Happy to do this before you're fully open if you want the content ready for launch."
