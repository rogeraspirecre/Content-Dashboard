# Brand Positioning

Brandon runs a two-audience model. Do not mix them up: flooding the feed with business-audience content is what tanked June (median fell to ~4.9K).

## Target audience
- **Primary (reach):** ambitious 20s–40s professionals and students making money/career decisions. Picking majors, switching careers, chasing their first $100K, worried about AI. Not real estate people; "how do I win the money game" people. This audience produced every 1M+ video.
- **Secondary (business):** commercial property owners and investors with underperforming buildings; the people who hire Aspire CRE. Small pool, low views, high dollar value.

## Client avatar (Brandon, on camera, Sep 2026 — use these words in ad targeting and pitch copy)
- **Landlord side:** business owners who own real estate. Entrepreneurial groups, usually based in Houston or Texas, who own buildings and need help leasing, managing, selling, or finding more.
- **Tenant side:** locally owned businesses that lease space.
- Not exclusive: they do work with out-of-state and out-of-Houston clients. The *main target* is locally based entrepreneurial owners.
- Note the gap: the avatar is entrepreneurial owners, not institutions. That's who the ads and the owner-education content should sound like.

## Offer
Done-for-you service: CRE brokerage via Aspire CRE (leasing, sales, representation). No course/coaching/community, by design. Content sells nothing right now; reach first, monetization handled separately (ads + future strategy).

## Pain points / desires
- **Reach audience:** more money, quit their job, pick the right path; anxieties: wrong career, AI risk, everyone online seems rich. They want a blunt scorekeeper who isn't selling them anything.
- **Business audience:** vacant building, quiet broker, "passive investment" that became a job. They want the property filled and a broker who hunts.

## Social proof
1. Practitioner, not guru: active broker, founder of Aspire CRE. "I make money from buildings, not from you" is the trust anchor vs course-sellers.
2. Real deal experience incl. unglamorous stories ($437 first deal) — reads more credible than flexes.
3. The audience itself: multiple 1M+ videos, top 8.1M.
4. To collect deliberately: brokerage client results/case studies for when monetization comes online.
