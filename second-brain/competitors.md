## Early findings from live pulls (Jul 7, 2026)

**Devin Jatho (24 recent reels sampled):** every post carries a comment-keyword CTA ("Comment GEAR / PROMPT / Songs for the free thing"). Reels with a comment-gate get 400–2,500 comments vs 4–19 without.

1. **Comment-keyword CTA** on 2–3 posts/week (e.g. "Comment DEAL and I'll send the checklist"). Directly juices the strongest ranking signal we see across both accounts.
2. **Loop-friendly endings** on ranking videos: end on a cliffhanger item that references the opener instead of a sign-off.

4. **Repost calendar**: winners get re-run 6–8 weeks later (proven: reposts pulled 38K–56K+ with zero new production).
